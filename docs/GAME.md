# How to play Luna Crew

## Goal

Keep the crew safe for **3 lunar days** (12 rounds). If Power, Life Support or Shield hits zero, the mission pauses and everyone rides home safe. That is not a fail screen. It is a tip for next time.

## Work points

Each round you get 10 work points (plus quiz bonuses, minus Pip rest penalties).

The four jobs do **not** have equal weights. A **Why?** button on each job shows the live number for this round.

| Job | Weight per point | What changes it | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Power | 5 in daytime, 1 at night. Damaged panels cut the gain in half, rounded down. | Day or night, and broken panels. | Night on the Moon lasts about two Earth weeks. [Moonlight](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/moonlight/) |
| Life Support | 4, and 1 Power for every 2 points. | The weight stays the same. Greenhouse lights add or remove Life Support on their own. | A life support system has to make air and water. [ECLSS](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/) |
| Shield | 4. Storm level N needs N times 20 Shield. | Storm level. Each point still adds 4. | A thick layer of Moon soil can protect a habitat during solar flares. [NASA Tech Briefs](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20130009378) |
| Science | 10 Mission Points. It does not fill the safety bars. | Nothing. | Learning points, not a safety bar. |

Before GO, each bar shows a lighter ghost of the predicted value. The briefing line at the top of the round says what matters most: a storm, broken panels, low life support, night, or planning ahead.

Greenhouse lights: on grows plants and Life Support but uses Power; off saves Power but Life Support drops.

**Ask Earth** costs 1 point and names the lowest bar after a short delay.

## Events

- **Rescue Pip**: spend points now to save an ice sample, or wait and lose the sample plus points next round.
- **Solar storm**: shelter (jobs pause) or keep working if the dirt shield is thick enough. Strong storms can damage panels.
- **Discovery**: study a find for Mission Points and a Life Support cost, or skip and stay focused on the base.
- **Repair**: tap glowing panel spots before the timer ends.

Story beats for rescue and discovery are made up for play. Storm dates and sources are from saved NASA files when available.

## After each lunar day

1. **Planner review**: your choice next to how real planners talk about the same risk. Not a score.
2. **Mission Card**: one sourced question. A correct answer adds 1 work point next round. A miss comes back later.

## Missions, stars and ranks

After crew setup, the Mission Map opens. Missions unlock in order. Full Mission is the three-day game. Mission of the Day and Challenge unlock after that.

| Stars | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Finished with every safety bar above zero. |
| 2 | Finished with Power, Life Support and Shield all above 25. |
| 3 | The lesson goal: finish, shelter, keep night power at 30 or more, bring the ice home, or leave the lights on. |

Ranks from total stars: Cadet, Junior Planner, Mission Planner, Senior Planner, Flight Director. Badges and journal cards are saved in this browser. Reset progress clears them. No account is used.

## Modes

- **Practice**: one guided round, then the real mission with fresh bars.
- **Demo** (`?demo=1`): storm moves earlier for presentations.
- **Same seed**: replay the same storm draw from the end screen.
- **Language**: English or Japanese any time in Settings.

## Accessibility

Settings cover sound, reduce motion, high contrast and text size. Dialogs trap focus. Escape closes dismissible screens. Icons mark every bar and job so color is never the only signal.
